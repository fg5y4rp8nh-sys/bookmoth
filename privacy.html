Privacy policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This page explains what information Bookmoth collects, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: we collect as little as possible and never sell it.

Purchases

Orders are processed by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the Merchant of Record. Lemon Squeezy collects your name, email address, billing country and payment details to complete the sale, deliver your download and handle taxes. We never see or store your full card details. Lemon Squeezy’s own privacy policy applies to that information.

From Lemon Squeezy we receive your name, email address, what you bought and when. We use it only to deliver your purchase, answer support requests and process refunds.

Emails

If you write to us or ask to be notified about new books, we keep your email address to reply or send that notice. We don’t send daily newsletters, and you can ask us to remove your address at any time by writing to products000001@gmail.com.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are hosted on our own site, so no third-party font service sees your visit. The checkout page, run by Lemon Squeezy, may use cookies needed to complete your payment.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run this site: Vercel (website hosting, which keeps basic technical logs such as IP address and browser type for security), FormSubmit (delivers the “notify me” form to our inbox) and Lemon Squeezy (checkout, payments and file delivery). They process data only to provide these services.

Children

Our books and this website are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email products000001@gmail.com and we’ll respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.
