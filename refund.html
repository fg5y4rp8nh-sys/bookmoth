Refund policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

We want every book to be worth your money. If it isn’t, you can have your money back.

14-day refund

If you’re not happy with a book, email products000001@gmail.com within 14 days of purchase and we’ll refund you in full. You don’t need to explain why.

How refunds are paid

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller and Merchant of Record. Refunds go back to the original payment method. Depending on your bank, the money usually appears within 5–10 business days.

After 14 days

Because our books are digital downloads, we can’t usually refund purchases older than 14 days. If something went wrong with your file, write to us anyway and we’ll make it right.
