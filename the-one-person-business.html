9

Price It Like You Mean It

New business owners almost always charge too little. It feels safer. It isn't.

Low prices cause problems you don't see at first. You need more customers to earn the same amount. You attract people who shop on price and leave for someone cheaper. You have no margin for slow months, taxes, or mistakes. And you get tired, which makes your work worse.
