4

The Big Three: Sun, Moon and Rising

If you learn only three things about your chart, learn these. Astrologers often call them "the big three," and together they give a surprisingly rounded first portrait.

The Sun: who you're becoming. The Sun represents your core identity, your sense of purpose, what makes you feel alive and proud. It's the self you grow into. Your Sun sign is the one you already know, determined by your birth date.

The Moon: what you need. The Moon represents your emotional nature: how you react, what comforts you, what you need to feel safe, and the habits you fall back on when you're tired or stressed. If the Sun is who you are at your best, the Moon is who you are at home in your pajamas.
