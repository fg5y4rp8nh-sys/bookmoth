3

Does Worrying Actually Help?

Let's look more closely at those positive beliefs about worrying, because they're often what keeps the habit going. Here are the most common ones, and what's really going on.

"Worrying helps me prepare." Planning helps you prepare. Worry is different. Planning is concrete and leads to action: you make a list, book an appointment, practice a speech. Worry repeats the same fears without moving forward. You can prepare without worrying.

"If I worry, I won't be caught off guard." Think back over your life. Did worrying about something in advance actually make it hurt less when it happened? Most people find it didn't. Instead, they suffered twice: once while worrying, and again when it happened. And most of the things they worried about never happened at all.
