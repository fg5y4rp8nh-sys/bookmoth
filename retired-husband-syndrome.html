12

Retire the Question “Where Are You Going?”

Ask almost any woman whose husband recently retired what drives her crazy, and this question will come up within the first minute. Where are you going? When will you be back? Who's going to be there? Can I come?

He probably doesn't mean it as surveillance. More often it's boredom, mild anxiety, or plain curiosity about a part of your life he never saw while he was working. For decades, your days were invisible to him. Now he notices every coming and going, and he doesn't know what to do with the information.

But being asked to account for your movements, again and again, feels like losing your independence one question at a time.
